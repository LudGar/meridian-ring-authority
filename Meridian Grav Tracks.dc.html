Grav tracks

Twenty-six Grav racing tracks: one in each of fifteen biomes, and eleven at the ring's key places, from Meridian Central to the underside of the hull. Every track is a small network of four sections. Sections join at three nodes, so one track gives four route sets, one per class, and each runs forward and in reverse.

§1 · How a track works
Every track uses this layout. A, B, C close the main loop; D is the cut back from J1. Orange with an arrow = one-way section (example).

Four sections, three nodes. S is the start and finish, with the pits and the grandstand. J1 and J2 are junctions with gates that marshals switch between races. A section is a stretch of track between two nodes, with its own ground, hazards and field.

One route set per class. Every track runs all four classes, each on its own combination of sections:

G-4 StreetA · DShort loop, the cut back from J1 G-3 SectionA · BSprint, S to J2, finish at J2 G-2 TrunkA · B · CGrand loop G-1 PrimeA · B · C · A · DLong: the grand loop, then the short loop

Reverse versions. Each route also runs backwards, giving 8 routes per track and 208 on the ring. Each track has one one-way section (a drop, jump or downwind run) that only works in one direction. Reverse routes swap it for its reverse version, a different line between the same two nodes, marked -R.

Vacuum tracks. Above the crest, on the outer face and under the hull there is no air. G-4 to G-2 craft run there with the sealed-canopy kit. The G-1 Prime craft is sealed as standard.

§5 · Signature tracks for 3D

Four tracks get full 3D models, one from each kind of place: GR-16 Central GP (city), GR-18 Wall Ascent (the wall), GR-23 Deep Caverns (underground) and GR-21 Hull Circuit (outside, in vacuum). Each model shows all four sections and both versions of the one-way section, and can highlight any route set: open the 3D models →

Drawing tracks in the CI: the track edge is a bone line with slate kerbs. Grav rails, where a track feeds the craft's lift, are teal. Race gates, junction switches and one-way arrows are orange. Section boards at every node carry the code in mono, e.g. GR-07 · B · J1→J2.
