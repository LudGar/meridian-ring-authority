RULES

ARROWS
Chamfered shaft, 90° head. Down arrow always means "the level below", never "straight ahead".
LEVELS
L-octagon at every stair, lift door and hatch. Graphite for L0–L3, orange for L4–L6.
SECTORS
Two digits, top-left of every sign. Sector numbers increase in the direction of spin.
BUILDING ID
Graphite plate at door height, to the right of the entrance.
EMERGENCY
Orange floor strips pulse towards the nearest P-05 access panel. Blue posts mark rescue points every 200 m.
MAINTENANCE
An orange ring on floor or hatch means maintenance access. A dashed ring means locked.
