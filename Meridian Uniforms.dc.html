Uniforms

FIVE SERVICE GROUPS · ONE CUT
COLOUR = JOB · STRIPE = RANK
→ LOGO SYSTEM

One garment pattern for everyone: a chamfered-collar jacket with a horizontal chest band at the height of the vehicles' teal line, an octagonal patch on the left shoulder and an ID plate on the chest. The base colour shows the job, the shoulder stripes show the rank. These are flat garment drawings, not final fashion illustration.

MAINTENANCE CREW
Signal-orange jacket, graphite trousers, slate hem band, reflective teal chest band. Hard hat and harness in L4–L6.
TRANSIT & PUBLIC SERVICE
Bone jacket matching the trams and stops, slate trousers, teal band. Worn by drivers, station staff and terminal attendants.
ENERGY & CORE SYSTEMS
Radiator-graphite jacket with a teal collar and teal patch. Antistatic fabric for L6 reactor and computing halls.
RESCUE & HYDROLOGY
Service-blue jacket with an orange high-visibility band, so it matches hydrants and emergency points. Fire, rescue and water crews.
EXTERIOR / VACUUM SUIT
Bone hard suit with an orange chest band, slate life-support module with a teal status strip, and a glass helmet. For the hull side and hatch chambers.
