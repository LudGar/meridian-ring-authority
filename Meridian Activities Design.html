Keeping the ring alive

What players do on Meridian. The ring is a machine that has to be kept running, and that is the core fantasy: fighting what goes wrong, doing the work that keeps it from going wrong, and getting around a world 31,416 km long.

§3 · Careers

Being anything

Each career is a full game loop of its own, the way a dedicated simulator is, and every one of them changes the shared world.

Maintenance technician

Repair panels, service crawlers and inspection robots, trace conduit faults.

like PowerWash Simulator, Car Mechanic, House Flipper

Transit and logistics

Drive the buses between the stops, haul freight on the expressway, run the trunk line.

like Trafficity, Euro Truck Simulator, train sims

Section steward

Plan and grow a section's city on its planned grid; budgets, services, roundabouts.

like Cities: Skylines

Farmer, fisher, diver

The agricultural zones, the lakes and seas, the underwater layer.

like Farming Simulator, fishing games

Spaceport crew and pilot

Air traffic control, shuttle runs over the rim, cargo contracts between rings.

like Elite Dangerous, flight sims

Leisure-village host

Cafés, hotels, beach clubs: the crews' free time is your business.

like restaurant and hotel management games

Crafter and gatherer

Bone ceramic, aged ceramic, slate, energy cells; recipes per era.

like FFXIV's crafting and gathering classes

Emergency services

Fire, rescue and police, dispatched from the call points every 200 m on public routes.

like emergency-service sims

Architect and designer

Housing and civic buildings in the chamfer-and-band style; signage and wayfinding.

like building and interior design games

§5 · Progression

One world, many paths

  • Resonance instead of levels (from the Design-Handbuch), kept per career. A technician and a raider progress side by side; neither path is the real one.
  • Section condition is shared world state. Neglect spawns incidents and hardens that section's shaft descent; repairs push it back; stewards' decisions change the city.
  • The careers feed each other. Raids drop reactor parts tuners want; a shaft descent needs a lift the technicians repaired; haulers supply the crafters; pilots fly groups out to the hull trials.
§6 · Build order

First slices

  1. Shaft descent. The spires and their descent already exist; walkable levels L0–L6 make the first dungeon.
  2. Vehicles and traffic. The Trafficity step: it carries transit, logistics, tuning and racing.
  3. One maintenance job loop, tied to section condition: the first career without weapons.
